SEC.01

How a thing gets here

Four stages
  1. STEP 01

    Spotted

    We find it on a campaign page or a trade floor and put it on the watch list. Nothing is for sale yet — you can ask us to email you when it opens.

  2. STEP 02

    Backed

    We put our own money into the campaign and take the first samples. Plenty of things stop here, and you never see them.

  3. STEP 03

    Shipping

    The production run landed. Your order goes out from our shelf in a day or two, not from a factory nine time zones away.

  4. STEP 04

    In stock

    It earned a permanent spot in the catalogue. Reorderable, supported, and stocked with the spares that wear out first.

On the bench right now

We buy the first run so you are not the test batch.

First production runs are where the problems live: the hinge that loosens, the app that never ships, the battery that lasts a third of what the page promised. We buy them, use them for a month, and only list what holds up. If something we sold goes bad later, we pull it and tell you why.
